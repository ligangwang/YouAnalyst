# AI map discovery

The map has five complementary discovery features:

- Select a company to centre it and fade unrelated stars (existing focus behavior).
- Click a company relationship line or its panel row to open its explanation and sources, then explore the other endpoint. Industry membership remains a role, not a business relationship.
- Guided journeys follow up to five actual sourced edges. A bounded search avoids cycles and tries to avoid dead ends; route length depends on published coverage.
- What's new lists and highlights company relationships first published in the last seven days. The company master projection passes through `publishedAt`. Existing undated relationships stay undated; source/report dates are never used as publication dates. Industry and import publishers timestamp newly created relationships without changing old records.
- Following is stored in `users/{uid}.followedCompanyIds`, capped at 200 and updated transactionally through authenticated `/api/map-follows`. No collection is created. The Following list returns users to companies, and new connections identify followed endpoints. This is in-app discovery, not email or push delivery. Anonymous users receive a contextual sign-in link and can follow after signing in.

No backfill is run by this change. Legacy undated relationships will not glow. Relationships, sources and company records are not generated by the journey UI.
