# AI map discovery

The map has five complementary discovery features:

- Select a company to centre it and fade unrelated stars (existing focus behavior).
- Click a company relationship line or its panel row to open its explanation and sources, then explore the other endpoint. Industry membership remains a role, not a business relationship.
- Guided journeys follow up to five actual sourced edges. A bounded search avoids cycles and tries to avoid dead ends; route length depends on published coverage.
- What's new lists and highlights company relationships first published in the last seven days. The company master projection passes through `publishedAt`. Existing undated relationships stay undated; source/report dates are never used as publication dates. Industry and import publishers timestamp newly created relationships without changing old records.
- Following is stored in `industry_map_preferences/{uid}.followedCompanyIds`, capped at 200 and updated transactionally through authenticated `/api/map-follows`. This existing collection is denied to direct client reads by Firestore rules; no collection is created. The Following list returns users to companies, and new connections identify followed endpoints. This is in-app discovery, not email or push delivery. Anonymous users receive a contextual sign-in link and can follow after signing in.

No backfill is run by this change. Legacy undated relationships will not glow. Relationships, sources and company records are not generated by the journey UI.

The 3D overview uses the original volumetric force layout and angled camera. Company label font sizes scale with camera distance (smaller at overview distance, larger nearby), and collision bounds scale with them. Company names take priority over tickers, and all nodes are eligible for collision-aware labels as the camera moves. Sector labels sit at the cluster centroid, scale with camera distance and focus the 3D neighborhood when clicked; nearby relationship types appear on closer zoom or company selection. Directional relationships include arrowheads; symmetric relationships use a bidirectional label. Labels open the existing evidence panel.

Search results and the collapsible company browser focus a company without filtering away its neighbors. Both use the already loaded graph, with no extra data requests. Rendering remains demand-driven, including an explicit invalidation when asynchronous HTML labels mount.

Company names are capped near 14px and tickers near 11px. Sector labels share the same bounded distance scaling. The selected relationship gets first placement priority, full type text, and a viewport-clamped label at extreme zoom/pan. Other edge labels still use collision culling.

### Map label identity and visibility

Sector labels use a light serif face, wider spacing, and sector color to distinguish them from company labels. Both retain bounded distance scaling in the 3D scene. Relationship labels and arrows require at least one endpoint company label to be placed in the current frame, including the selected edge. The first company label gets priority, then its relationship labels, sector labels, and remaining companies/relationships.

Company records may contain `names.en` and `names.zh-CN`. The map API includes these fields and string aliases. Graph labels, search results, the company panel, relationship endpoints, following, and the expandable directory use the selected locale, falling back to the canonical `name` if a localized name is missing. Search matches both names and aliases in either locale.

`data/ai-supply-chain/company-names.json` covers all 132 companies present on the live AI Industry Map on September 14, 2026. It is not a translation of the entire company directory. Every entry has `en` and `zh-CN`; international brands without an established Chinese display name retain their brand in both languages. Company websites, issuer disclosures and company/industry profiles are recorded per entry. Names are display brands/short names, not replacements for legal names. `Publish reviewed company names` previews or atomically writes names, name evidence and the search index to existing `companies` documents. It refuses missing/changed identities and conflicting translations; no collection, company, canonical name or relationship is created or replaced.
